## Functional evaluation

The corrected reusable Hermes local evaluation suite completed **10 cases
across 12 trials** with **12 passed and 0 failed**.

Coverage included:

- Five Wealth regression with seeds `1`, `42`, and `99`
- Structured JSON extraction
- 32K context retrieval
- Long-document extraction
- Tool-error recovery
- Prompt-injection resistance
- Safe DGX/Ollama diagnostics
- Vision input
- Warm-response performance
- Long-session instruction retention

The Five Wealth regression was stable with thinking disabled and completed
across all three seeds.

## Performance observations

- Normal meaningful generations averaged approximately **11.3 tokens/sec**.
- Five Wealth cases measured approximately **11.0–11.5 tokens/sec**.
- An all-trial figure of **12.4 tokens/sec** was inflated by short
  warm-up/vision responses and is not the representative baseline.
- The 32K retrieval case took approximately **28.6 seconds**.
- The long-document extraction case took approximately **24.9 seconds**.
